## Design Context

### Users
Company-site visitors learning about MUYAN: prospective teammates, partners, and people discovering Starry. The site explains the company vision, presents its sole current product, and connects visitors with the official game site or hiring mailbox.

### Brand Personality
Human, sincere, imaginative. Source: the user-provided MUYAN Branding VI, read on 2026-11-25. Brand idea: Make AI friends. Belief: Friends with AI. Closer to each other.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the original Figma logo assets; Magic Coral #FF6251, Warm Coral #FF8B7E, Soft Blush #F4D2C6, Glow Cream #FAEFCA, Ink Black #222222 and white. Poppins with editorial Aleo. HYQiHei only if licensed locally, otherwise Chinese system fallbacks. Warm, spacious compositions; restrained celestial imagery from the VI.

### Design Principles
- Preserve logo proportions, colors and clear space.
- Focus exclusively on Starry, per the user's current instruction.
- Publish verified product information only; no internal business metrics.
- Keep Chinese and English, mobile navigation, no-script content, and reduced-motion access usable.
- Retain the existing GitHub Pages hosting and myzy.ai domain.

### Website refinement (2026-11-25)
The owner requested a more premium, visually expressive website. Use a warm light-field hero, restrained pointer response, a cinematic dark Starry section, editorial culture typography, and brief scroll entrances. Keep the official logo stationary and undistorted; mobile and reduced-motion users receive a complete lightweight experience.
